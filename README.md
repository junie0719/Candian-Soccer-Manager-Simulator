# Canadian Soccer Manager Simulator

##Play the Game on itch.io
https://junie0719.itch.io/canadian-football-manager

A 2D football management simulation game independently developed in Unity and C#. Inspired by Football Manager, the project combines real-time football simulation, autonomous player AI, tactical decision-making, squad management, and league progression.

## Gameplay Features

### Real-Time Match Simulation
- 11v11 AI-controlled football matches.
- Attribute-based player movement and decision-making.
- Intelligent passing, through balls, and interception detection.
- Shooting accuracy influenced by distance, pressure, and player attributes.
- Defensive positioning and intelligent man-marking.
- Goalkeeper AI and ball distribution.
- Kickoffs, throw-ins, corners, and goal kicks.
- Live scoreboard, match timer, and halftime.
- Individual player shirt numbers.

### Tactical Management
- Six selectable formations: 4-3-3, 4-4-2, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, 5-3-2, and 3-4-3.
- Live formation changes during matches.
- Individual player position swapping.
- Position-specific player ratings.
- Natural and assigned player positions.
- In-game tactical interface with pause and resume functionality.

### Squad Management
- 22-player squads with starting and reserve players.
- Starting XI selection.
- Individual player attributes and market values.
- Live substitutions with a five-substitution limit.
- Prevention of substituted player re-entry.
- Player stamina tracking.
- Confirmation and cancellation of pending tactical changes.

### League Management
- Eight-team Canadian football league.
- Full 14-matchday season.
- Automated round-robin fixture generation.
- Dynamic league standings and match results.
- Attribute-based match simulation.
- Ability to play or simulate matches.
- Ability to simulate the remainder of an ongoing match while preserving the current score.
